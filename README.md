One image summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in world news: **40 sourced
stories** across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific,
business, tech & science and sport — from two tankers hit by naval mines in
the Strait of Hormuz and a new US tariff proposal, to Shenzhou-24 carrying the
first foreign astronaut to Tiangong and a fuel-tanker fire on Cairo's ring
road that killed at least 30.

The front page carries the full date in its masthead, a UTC timeline of the
day's key moments, and a world map marking the day's flashpoints. Every story
is tagged with its source outlet, and the footer states the story count and a
date check: all stories were first reported on 25 November 2026 (UTC).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) draws the page deterministically
— two runs write byte-identical `entry.png` (1024×1024). Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Sources: AP, Reuters, SCMP, Xinhua, Jerusalem Post, Just Security,
Al Jazeera, UEFA.
